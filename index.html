How the workflow works

  1. Find a niche or enter your own topic.
  2. Search Amazon manually and choose a product.
  3. Paste the product name and affiliate link.
  4. Generate the title, description, keywords, disclosure, and image brief.
  5. Run the checklist and log performance.

Important boundaries

This app does not sign into Amazon, search Amazon, scrape listings, create images, or promise income. It generates planning and copy from the information you enter.

Always verify Amazon Associates rules, Pinterest rules, product claims, and disclosure placement before publishing.
